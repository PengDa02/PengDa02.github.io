01 / Introduction

About me

Hi, I'm Da Peng, a Ph.D. student at Xi'an Jiaotong University, advised by Prof. Wei Ke. My research focuses on large vision language models and native vision agents.

More broadly, I am interested in artificial intelligence and machine learning, particularly in enabling models to better connect and reason across vision, language, and other modalities. Currently, my research is focused on physical perception in real-world environments, where I explore simple yet effective approaches for improving model understanding and reasoning.

I have been fortunate to collaborate with many talented researchers, including Zonghao Guo, Yansong Li, Shichu Sun, Jiale Wang, Xuesong Yang, and Yichen Zhang. This list will continue to grow over time.

Feel free to reach out if you are interested in discussing research ideas or potential collaborations.
